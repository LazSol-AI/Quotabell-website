Short description

Quotabell shows Claude, ChatGPT / Codex, Grok and Antigravity CLI plan limits on your iPhone and widgets, with percent left and reset countdowns.

Long description

Quotabell is a free v1 iPhone app for keeping an eye on AI plan limits. It brings reported 5-hour and weekly limits, remaining percentages and reset countdowns into one dashboard and Home and Lock Screen widgets. Grok / SuperGrok has a weekly pool only through the main data source, and Antigravity CLI limits are shown per reported quota group. Multiple accounts can have their own nicknames, and optional local alerts warn at 20% and 5% left.

The default, safer data mode uses a free Mac menu bar helper to ask the official CLIs for usage, without reading or storing provider tokens. Quota data and related display metadata sync through the user’s private iCloud. An optional phone-only mode signs in as the providers’ official CLI apps (Google sign-in for Antigravity) and keeps tokens in the iPhone Keychain; it carries provider-policy and account-access risks. Optional Wake on the Mac sends a tiny message through the user’s own Claude or Codex CLI and uses a small amount of their plan. There are no LAZSOL accounts or app servers, ads, analytics or tracking.
