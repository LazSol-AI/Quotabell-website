How it works

Two ways to read usage.

Choose for each account. The Mac helper is the default and the safer option.

Default · safer

Mac helper

Your Mac reads the numbers. Your sign-ins stay there.

  1. Install the free menu bar helper and sign in to the provider’s official CLI: claude, codex, grok or agy.
  2. The helper asks the CLI for usage. It never reads or stores provider tokens.
  3. Your private iCloud carries the readings to your iPhone and widgets. Use the same Apple Account on both.
Opt-in

Phone-only

No Mac needed, with a risk to understand first.

  1. Sign in on the provider’s own page in Apple’s sign-in sheet, as the provider’s official CLI app.
  2. Tokens stay in the iPhone Keychain, device-only unless you turn on iCloud Keychain sync.
  3. Requests go only to that provider’s domains. Sign Out deletes the account’s tokens.

This is not a provider-approved integration and can lead to account restrictions. The app shows a risk sheet before you opt in. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Questions, answered.

Which limits can I see?

Claude and ChatGPT / Codex show the 5-hour session and weekly limits reported for your plan, with percent left and reset countdowns when available. Claude model-specific weekly limits appear when reported. Grok / SuperGrok has a weekly pool only.

Antigravity CLI shows the 5-hour and weekly limits it reports for each quota group; an account with only a weekly limit shows weekly only. Quotabell never invents a limit the provider does not report.

Do I need a Mac?

The default, safer mode uses the free Mac helper, the provider’s official CLI and the same Apple Account with iCloud on your Mac and iPhone. You can instead opt in to phone-only sign-in for individual accounts after reading its risk disclosure.

Is Quotabell free?

Version 1 is free, including the Mac helper. There are no ads, analytics or tracking, and no account to create with LAZSOL. Your provider’s plan and terms still apply.

How often does it update?

The Mac checks every five minutes by default, with back-off after failures. The iPhone refreshes when opened and when iOS permits background work, and iOS schedules widget refreshes. Check the “updated” time and the provider’s own usage page when timing matters.

Can I add Work and Personal accounts?

Yes. Add several accounts per provider and give each a nickname. Phone-only accounts have separate Keychain sign-ins. A Mac account uses the CLI’s current sign-in; for Claude, Codex and Grok you can also point an account at a separate CLI config folder, which Quotabell passes to the CLI without opening. Antigravity on the Mac always uses the current agy sign-in.

Does checking usage send a chat?

No. Normal checks do not chat or run models. Wake is the only exception: if you turn it on, the Mac sends one tiny message through your own Claude or Codex CLI at a time you choose, which uses a small amount of your plan. It is off by default and offers up to three scheduled times a day per Mac account.
